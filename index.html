This is FOCUSdrink, a minimalistic pomodoro focus timer designed to help keep workflow manageable by slicing it up into manageable chunks with breaks in between.
This app is very heavily inspired by the Flow widget on MacOS, "lovingly crafted by Yugen, a tiny but mighty indie developer based in Frankfurt, Germany."
